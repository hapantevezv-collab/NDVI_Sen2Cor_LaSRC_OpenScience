## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-21

First public release, accompanying the manuscript submitted to *Remote Sensing* (MDPI).

- Canonical analysis contract: 1,810 exact-date matches → 1,809 after cross-product QC → **1,797** common observations (≥10 valid pixels in L1C and LaSRC), **106** management polygons, **22** farms (2019: 668; 2020: 1,129). Retires the earlier 91-polygon / 1,809-record dataset.
- Farm-cluster bootstrap (B = 2,000, seed 2026) for all confidence intervals; annual (2019, 2020) and pooled agreement tables.
- Land-use stratification: 1,527 observations, 88 polygons, 21 farms (UT0 excluded). Masking sensitivity: 2,585 paired records (strict subset 2,521).
- Acceptance tests (expected counts and metrics, tolerance 1e-12): PASS. Canonical outputs promoted to `data/derived/` with SHA-256 checksums.
- Final figures regenerated by script: study-area map (Figure 1), processing workflow (Figure 2), three-pipeline NDVI comparison for farm 3948 (Figure 3), agreement diagnostics (Figure 4), land-use gradient (Figure 5).
- Study-area extent corrected to 854 ha (dissolved farm boundaries).
- References verified against original sources; sen2lasrc cited as v2.0.2 (10.5281/zenodo.15007856).
- Author metadata completed (ORCID 0000-0003-1797-0057, two affiliations); generative-AI use declared.
