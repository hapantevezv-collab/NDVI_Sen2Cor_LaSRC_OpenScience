# Scientific provenance

Version 1.0.0 (2026-10-21). All counts below are read from the canonical outputs that passed the acceptance tests.

## Study design

- Study area: Colombian Orinoco Piedmont, Department of Meta; 22 farms, 854 ha (dissolved farm boundaries).
- Spatial units: 2019 SCR inventory, 143 management-polygon features defining 141 management polygons (`farm_polygon__UT` key).
- Sentinel-2 tiles: 18NXJ and 18NXK (UTM zone 18N). Comparison period: 2019-01-01 to 2020-12-31 (exact-date matching).
- Analytical chain: 1,810 exact-date matches → 1,809 after cross-product QC (one record excluded; exclusion ledger) →
  **1,797** common observations with ≥ 10 valid 10 m pixels in L1C and LaSRC (12 records removed; 107 → **106** polygons; **22** farms).
  2019: 668 observations; 2020: 1,129. Polygon-date duplicates within a pipeline were excluded, not averaged.
- Category-stratified set: **1,527** observations, 88 polygons, 21 farms (six SCR categories UT2–UT8; UT0 excluded, −270).
- Paired masking-sensitivity set: **2,585** polygon-by-date records (128 polygons, 20 farms); strict subset (≥ 10 valid pixels in both LaSRC exports): 2,521.

## Inputs

- Input-scene manifest: 708 scene records (L1C 157, Sen2Cor 175, LaSRC 376 outputs).
- Auxiliary-data manifest: 217 MODIS_FUSED files retrieved through the USGS M2M API.
- Geometry manifest: `geometry_manifest.json` (geometries themselves are restricted and not distributed).

## Processing pipelines

1. **L1C/TOA:** Copernicus Data Space via EODAG 3.10.2; 1000-DN offset removed for processing baseline ≥ 04.00; Fmask 4.6 + s2cloudless mask; local zonal mean with rasterio at 10 m.
2. **Sen2Cor L2A/SR:** `COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED` accessed through the Google Earth Engine Python API from JupyterLab; SCL classes 1, 3, 8–11 excluded; `reduceRegions` on the native B8 10 m grid.
3. **LaSRC L2A/SR:** `sen2lasrc` 2.0.2 (LaSRC 3.2.0) in the digest-pinned `lasrc-fmask` Docker image (full digest in `environment/container-provenance.txt`); MODIS_FUSED auxiliaries; INT16-scaled reflectance; local zonal mean at 10 m.
- L1C and LaSRC share one Fmask 4.6 + s2cloudless mask generated by `sen2lasrc` at 20 m and resampled to 10 m (valid = Fmask clear and s2cloudless clear).

## Statistical analyses

Mean paired difference, RMSD, uRMSD, Lin's CCC, Deming regression (λ = 1), and Bland–Altman limits of agreement (± 1.96 SD); R² reported descriptively.
Confidence intervals are **farm-cluster** bootstrap percentile intervals (B = 2,000, seed 2026): farms are resampled with replacement and all polygons and dates of each selected farm are retained.

## Acceptance

Expected counts and metrics checked with tolerance 1e-12 → PASS (`acceptance_report.json`). Canonical tables in `data/derived/` with SHA-256 checksums.
