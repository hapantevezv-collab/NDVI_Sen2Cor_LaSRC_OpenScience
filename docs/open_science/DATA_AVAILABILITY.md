# Data availability and governance

## Publicly releasable materials

- Derived summary tables used in the article.
- Analysis and visualization code without credentials or private paths.
- Final publication figures.
- Manuscript source, bibliography, and reproducibility documentation.
- Metadata, data dictionaries, provenance, checksums, and environment inventories.

## Materials not included in the public release

Farm-boundary and management-polygon geometries are governed by the Sustainable Cattle Ranching project and are not publicly distributed. Raw or locally cached Sentinel-2 imagery, USGS atmospheric auxiliary files, credentials, private Earth Engine assets, and third-party source data are also excluded unless redistribution rights are confirmed.

The public package documents how authorized users can reproduce the workflow without exposing farm identifiers, governed geometries, credentials, or private paths.

## Access requests

Requests for governed source data must follow applicable SCR data-governance and institutional authorization procedures. The Zenodo record must not imply guaranteed access by the author.
